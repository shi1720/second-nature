## Title

SECOND NATURE | Make Every Second Grow | A Procedural Blender Film

## Description

One drop. A world in motion. A reason to arrive.

SECOND NATURE is a 24-second Blender film about purposeful speed. A luminous amber drop races through precision gates and a vertical loop to reach a dry seed. The machine gives way to a held breath, a fall and a flower.

Entrant and project lead: Shivam Gupta.

Watch the film and explore its production studio:
https://second-nature-studio.web.app

Source, editable scene and workflow notes:
https://github.com/shi1720/second-nature

Created for the RenderJuice Blender Animation & Programmatic Rendering Contest 2026. Blender Python constructs the scene, geometry, materials, motion paths, camera edit and staggered petal unfolding. Motion is baked into keyframes. The soundtrack is original mathematical synthesis with no recorded samples.

The original film was rendered locally at 1920 x 1080: 576 frames, 24 fps, exactly 24 seconds. The separate studio offers controlled palette and soil-detail variants. Its complete Blue hour cloud production passed independent 24-second, 576-frame, 540p delivery checks, authenticated browser playback and matching MP4/Blender downloads. Cancellation and resume preserved the first 17 frames. Detailed evidence is included in the source repository.

AI and asset disclosure: OpenAI Codex assisted with concept development, code, production automation, documentation and review. Geometry, materials and synthesized audio were generated for this project. Typography uses Space Grotesk under SIL OFL 1.1. Tools include Blender, Python, FFmpeg, NumPy and Pillow. No stock models, image textures, recorded music or generative video were used in this film. Motion is scripted animation, not a physical simulation. No RenderJuice cloud rendering or sponsor endorsement is claimed.

Make every second grow.
