Open https://second-nature-studio.web.app. Play the featured 24-second film immediately. The Blue hour study and captioned studio guide demonstrate the actual workflow without consuming render capacity.

To test a new production, continue as a guest or create an email workspace with a password of at least 12 characters. Enter a production name, choose a palette and detail seed, then save. Refresh to confirm persistence. Select Create film when capacity is available. Blender builds, renders all 576 frames and assembles the 24-second 540p movie in the cloud. A full CPU production can take over an hour and continues when the tab is closed. After completion, play the custom variant and download its MP4 and editable Blender scene.

One worker is shared. Daily render-start limits are 3 globally, 2 per account and 2 per network, resetting at 00:00 UTC. Failed, cancelled and resumed starts count. Use cancellation only if needed; resume reuses valid saved frames. These admission limits are not a monetary billing cap. Exports and frame caches expire after seven days, so download files to keep. Guest-to-email linking retains the workspace; signing out of an unlinked guest can lose access. No visitor API key or payment is required.

Detailed reproducible checks and live acceptance evidence: https://github.com/shi1720/second-nature/blob/main/docs/TESTING.md
